# Tell Me About Yourself — Improved Scripts

Practice both. Use **Version A** for HR / first round.  
Use **Version B** if they want a short technical intro.

---

## Version A — Full intro (about 90–110 seconds)
Best for: “Tell me about yourself”

> Good morning, sir/ma’am.  
> I’m **Narendra Kumar**, from Andhra Pradesh. I’m pursuing **B.Tech CSE at SRM University AP** with a CGPA of **8.51**, and I’m an **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner**.  
>  
> I come from a simple family — my father is a farmer, my mother is a homemaker, and I have one elder brother. Their support is a big reason I keep pushing myself.  
>  
> In college, I focused on building real products, not only theory. My main project is **Nyay Sahayak**, an AI legal assistant that uses **RAG** to answer questions from the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita**, with role-based access and IPC–BNS mapping for **550+ sections**. I also built **GeoVerse AI**, a world-exploration app with live APIs and AI itineraries, and **AWS Intelligence Loop**, a serverless sentiment pipeline on AWS that processes **10,000+ records a day**.  
>  
> Outside academics, I have done **six national hackathons**, which improved my teamwork and speed under pressure. My hobby is **nature photography** — I run an Instagram page for my photos. It trains my eye for detail, and I bring that same detail-check habit into debugging and UI work.  
>  
> I’m looking for a role where I can learn from strong engineers, grow in backend/full-stack or AI-backed products, and contribute from day one. Thank you.

### Why this is better than your old one
- Still personal, but not too long on family
- Projects have one clear point each (not only names)
- Hobby is connected to work skill (detail + creativity)
- Ends with what you want + what you give

## Version B — Short tech intro (60–75 seconds)
Best for: technical / coding rounds

> Hi, I’m **Narendra Kumar**, final-year CSE at SRM University AP, CGPA **8.51**, AWS Cloud Practitioner.  
> I build with **Python, FastAPI, React, and AWS**.  
> My strongest project is **Nyay Sahayak** — a production RAG legal assistant using ChromaDB and Groq, with role-based workflows and IPC↔BNS mapping.  
> I’ve also shipped **GeoVerse AI** and an **AWS serverless sentiment pipeline** for 10k+ records/day.  
> I enjoy turning ideas into working products, and I’m looking for an SDE / backend / full-stack role where I can keep learning and contribute.

---

## Delivery tips
- Smile once at the start
- Pause after each project name (don’t rush)
- Do **not** memorize like a robot — keep meaning, change small words
- If time is short, use Version B only

## Practice checklist
- [ ] Version A under 2 minutes
- [ ] Version B under 75 seconds
- [ ] Can continue into Nyay Sahayak when they ask “tell me more”
