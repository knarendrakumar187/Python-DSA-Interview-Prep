# DAY 14 — Full Mock Interview Script
Do with a friend, or record yourself.

Total time: **60–75 minutes**

---

## Part 1 — Intro (2 min)
Say your intro from `Resume_Prep/INTRO.md`.

Score yourself:
- Clear? Calm? Under 75 sec?

## Part 2 — DSA (30–35 min)
Pick any 2:
1. Two Sum
2. Valid Parentheses
3. Reverse Linked List
4. Max Depth of Binary Tree
5. Longest Substring Without Repeating

For each:
- Clarify
- Example
- Approach
- Code
- Complexity

## Part 3 — Project grill (15–20 min)
Ask yourself / friend:
1. Explain Nyay Sahayak architecture
2. What is RAG? Why needed?
3. What was your hardest bug?
4. Explain AWS pipeline latency claim
5. What would you improve next?

## Part 4 — CS quick fire (10 min)
1. Primary key vs foreign key
2. Index — why and downside
3. Process vs thread
4. TCP vs UDP
5. OOP polymorphism example in Python
6. Write a SQL JOIN

## Part 5 — HR (5–8 min)
1. Tell me about yourself
2. Strength / weakness
3. Conflict in team (STAR)
4. Why should we hire you?

## After mock
Write 5 weak answers and fix them same day.
